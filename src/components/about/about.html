WHO WE ARE

Committed to Quality Livestock & Sustainable Farming

Novera Agro Farm is a modern livestock and dairy farming company dedicated to sustainable animal husbandry and high-quality livestock production. We focus on providing healthy, productive, and disease-free animals for farmers and commercial clients.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support farmers with high-quality cattle, goats, and buffalo while promoting modern and sustainable farming practices in Bangladesh.

Our Vision

We aim to become a leading agro-livestock brand by ensuring quality livestock supply and contributing to the growth of the agricultural economy.

  • Buffalo farming

  • Farmer support and consultancy
